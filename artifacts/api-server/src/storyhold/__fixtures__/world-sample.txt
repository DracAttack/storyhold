Mara Vale arrived at Stone Harbor before the storm. Mara Vale carried the brass key, although Mara Vale did not know which door it opened.

Captain Orin watched Mara Vale cross the market. Captain Orin had promised the Lantern Guild that Mara Vale would be protected, but the Lantern Guild no longer trusted Captain Orin.

Stone Harbor closes its sea gates whenever the black tide rises. No ship may leave Stone Harbor until the bell tower sounds three times. The rule was older than Captain Orin and older than the Lantern Guild.

Mara Vale found the first mark beneath the bell tower. Mara Vale told Captain Orin about the mark, but Captain Orin concealed the message from the Lantern Guild. By nightfall, Mara Vale understood that someone inside Stone Harbor wanted the sea gates kept shut.
